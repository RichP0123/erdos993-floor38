## Project work

Rich Patterson directed the project through sustained coordination of the mathematical objective, scope, available resources, review requirements and publication preparation.

Mathematical development, Lean and supporting-code implementation, debugging, build orchestration, source/proof audits and documentation involved substantial generative-AI assistance, including ChatGPT/Codex in the retained development workflow. No complete attribution to particular historical models is inferred where the record does not identify them. AI-assisted reviews are not independent human referee endorsements. The publisher remains responsible for the claims and release.

The work reported here connects actual graph counting to rooted-state closure and exact certificate soundness, removes the older inherited census premise, assembles the finite forest theorem, records its verification provenance, and provides reusable Mathlib interfaces. These contributions do not imply invention of every mathematical ingredient or ownership of all predecessor expression.

The commissioned FLOOR208 research round was part of this project. Its retained prompt and return document the finite-difference certificate approach, rooted/bundle state construction and complete certificate package. AUDIT213 reviewed the returned method; AUDIT215 regenerated the catalogue and replayed the certificates; later Lean work established the unconditional graph theorem. These are distinct stages of the project's work. The exact seat/provider or model for the FLOOR208 return is not preserved, so no particular human or model is credited by inference.
