## Exact custom-loader method

The complete final applications use a native Lean helper because ordinary full-environment finalization exceeded the available memory in the initial workflow. The helper retains a selected dictionary of exact original `ConstantInfo` objects, including their proof bodies, while retaining unchanged full original module data and imports.

It checks dependency closure, original ownership and axiom-cache coverage. Missing cached theorem information requires proof-body closure. It does not replace proved declarations with axioms. It submits the new proof terms to Lean's normal kernel declaration checker with `debug.skipKernelTC=false`, and an intentionally ill-typed proof of `False` must be rejected.

The saved final object is reimported in a fresh process with the **same custom exact loader**. The check requires theorem kind, the literal independently compiled target, the expected universe parameters, standard axioms and a newly kernel-checked alias. Object and executable identities are pinned across the checks.

Only a small pilot also passed ordinary Lean reimport and comparison with standard finalization. Neither complete final-object reimport is represented as ordinary full Lean import. The actual full checks and their retained declarations remain open to focused external scrutiny.

## Trust and interpretation

An axiom list alone is insufficient. The graph definitions must count the intended independent sets; the exact proposition must be checked; the kernel must actually reject invalid proof terms; and source/object/import provenance and loader semantics must agree. The [paper](PAPER.md) explains the mathematical connection.

Installed revision-locked Lean and Mathlib compiled libraries are the stated upstream trust boundary. A full independent rebuild of all their sources is not claimed. The source ZIP's hashes and read-back establish packaging integrity, not another proof compilation.

No independent-machine full reproduction, external human referee endorsement, unrestricted Erdős 993 theorem or Lean-verified ceiling is claimed. See [review questions](REVIEW.md), [reproduction](REPRODUCTION.md) and [fixed artifact identities](ARTIFACTS.md).
