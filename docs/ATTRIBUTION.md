## FLOOR208 provenance and limits

FLOOR208 was a commissioned idea-generation round within this project. The saved AUDIT207 checkpoint identifies the next FLOOR208 prompt; that prompt requests a new complete exclusion method and source/certificate archive; the retained FLOOR208 return supplies the finite-difference method and named deliverables. AUDIT213 reviews the returns, and AUDIT215 records the later archive intake and local replay. This is an in-project research and review chain, not an archive of unknown project origin.

The retained records do not identify the exact seat/provider or historical model responsible for the return. No human author or model is assigned by guessing from sandbox paths. Separate solver and checker implementations describe a verification arrangement, not independent human authorship. Those limits do not erase the documented project origin or its methodological credit.

The final formalization proves actual graph correspondence, bank closure, interval/coverage soundness and the concrete finite obligations, removing the earlier inherited smaller-order baseline. This is distinct from the earlier computational replay and does not erase its methodological credit.

The complete Lean source archive does not separately distribute the FLOOR208 round's original C++, Python or report files. The project-wide license proposal still awaits Rich Patterson's adoption and a confirmed covered file scope; the missing seat identifier is not presented as an established outside-archive licensing blocker. [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) records the distribution boundary. Private acquisition paths and attachment identifiers are not part of this public provenance account.
