# Source notices and license scope

This notice records attribution and distribution scope; it does not create a project-wide license grant.

The verified proof-source archives preserve their selected original source bytes and existing notices. The proposed license for original project contributions is Apache-2.0, subject to an authorized grant with an accurately established file scope. Existing third-party notices retain their own scope. Citation metadata intentionally does not declare a completed blanket license.

Lean and Mathlib are credited and retain their own license terms. Installed upstream source trees and compiled caches are prerequisites rather than bundled copies in the complete proof-source archive. If dependency files are redistributed separately, their applicable notices and license terms must also be preserved.

The complete source archive retains its existing Mathlib Apache-2.0 notice as `licenses/2_LICENSE` and the historical NetworkX BSD three-clause notice as `licenses/1_FINITE_BASE_NETWORKX_LICENSE.txt`. NetworkX is not required by the portable Lean rebuild. Neither notice licenses unrelated project files.

The FLOOR208 method and reproduction package arose from this project's commissioned research round. Its original C++/Python/report files are not separately included in the complete Lean source archive. The saved prompt, return and audits establish the project origin; the exact seat/provider or model is not identified in those records. We credit the round without inventing an author or a separate license grant. Missing seat metadata is not evidence that this was an unrelated outside archive or an established third-party licensing blocker.

Earlier papers and external repository trees are cited rather than reproduced as part of this documentation. No cited researcher or software project is represented as endorsing the result.

See [attribution](docs/ATTRIBUTION.md) and [contributions](CONTRIBUTORS.md). A final license decision must identify the grantor and the covered files explicitly; the current license proposal is not such a decision.
